# Website navigation

- **Tasks** — discover engineering tasks and contribute or revise a task.
- **Docs** — browser, CLI and local-agent usage.
- **Contributors** — community profiles.
- **Account** — sign in and manage your contributions.

Task submission has five steps: About the task, Task description, Revision,
Human knowledge and Final review. Saved drafts allow contributors to return to earlier steps.

Published pages show readable descriptions and available files. Private input
history is accessible to the contributor and administrators.

Benchmark-related pages remain under development.
